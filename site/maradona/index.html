The Man Who Became God

Here in Brazil, one of the greatest tributes you can receive is becoming an inspiration for a carnival samba.
To promote Maradona's series around here, we decided to pay this tribute to our biggest football rival.

Diego Maradona had a lifelong passion for Brazil.
Especially for Carnival.

And on his birthday, his series premiered in Brazil.
The occasion called for a gift no other country
could deliver: a samba-enredo.
