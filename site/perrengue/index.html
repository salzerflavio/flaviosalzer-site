TROUBLE CALLS FOR TROUBLE

Once you leave your house to buy beer, anything can happen.
It's better to order it from the Zé Delivery app.

Role
Art Direction, Concept
With
Bruno Brux, Ju Utsch, Sofia Calvit, Cauique Johnson, Deborah Ferreira, Vitinho Chagas, Fill Almeida, Gheisa Lessa
Agency
GUT
Client
Zé Delivery
Year
2023

Zé Delivery was made to make your life easier,
some people still insist on trouble.

Since we got tired of explaining that you don’t need to leave the house to buy beer anymore, we created the "Trouble Calls For Trouble" platform.

Where we literally drew it out to show that,
in the 21st century, there’s no reason to
suffer just to buy warm beer.
